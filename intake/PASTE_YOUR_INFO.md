# Paste your information here

This is the drop box. Dump everything you have about your career into the
sections below, in any format, then tell your terminal agent:

    /intake

(or, if your agent has no skills: paste the contents of `prompts/intake.md`)

The agent reads this file and converts it into structured master data in
`data/*.yaml`. After that, this file has done its job. Nothing here is ever
rendered onto a resume directly.

## How to fill this in

**Do not write it like a resume.** Write it like a brain dump. Long, messy,
repetitive, and over-detailed is exactly right. The whole system is built on
the idea that master data should be far richer than any single resume, so the
tailoring step has real material to choose from.

Fast ways to fill this:

- Paste your current resume, in full
- Paste your LinkedIn profile: open your profile, select all, paste
- Paste old job descriptions from roles you actually held
- Paste performance review text, brag documents, project READMEs
- Paste a Slack message where you explained what you did to someone

Then go back and add the numbers. Numbers are what the engine protects and
what recruiters read. For every accomplishment, try to answer: how many, how
much, how fast, compared to what, and who saw it.

**One rule that matters:** everything you write here must be true. The
validator can stop the AI from inventing facts. It cannot stop you. If a
number is a guess, mark it as a guess and it will be flagged as CLAIMED
instead of VERIFIED.

- "My GPA is 3.549. Resumes say 3.5. Never round it to 3.6."
